# FUTURE_DS_03 – Marketing Funnel & Conversion Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing marketing funnel and lead conversion data to understand how users move from visitors → leads → customers.

The objective of this analysis is to identify drop-off points, evaluate campaign performance, and generate actionable insights that help improve conversion rates and business growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze traffic-to-lead conversion
- Measure lead-to-customer conversion rates
- Identify funnel drop-off stages
- Compare performance across channels and campaigns
- Generate recommendations to improve conversions
